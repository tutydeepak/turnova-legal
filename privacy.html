Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Turnova lets you join a barber shop or salon queue from your phone. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email, or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • Phone number: if you provide one, so the shop can call you if you are late. Only staff of the shop you joined can see it.
  • Queue activity: the shops you join, services and barber you choose, and the times you join, are called, and finish.
  • Ratings and reviews: stars and optional comments you submit after a visit.
  • Favourites and preferences: shops you favourite and the preferences you choose in the app (such as area and language).
  • Device token: an identifier used to send you queue notifications.

Location

If you tap "Use my location", the app uses your phone's location only to sort shops by distance on your device. Your location is never uploaded or stored by us.

How we use your information

  • To run the queue, show your position and estimated wait, and notify you when it is your turn.
  • To let shops manage their queue and contact you if you are late.
  • To show shop ratings and your visit history.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Who can see what

  • Other customers see a masked version of your name in a queue.
  • Shop staff see the names and details of people in their own shop's queue.
  • Reviews you write may be shown with your first name or a masked name.

Services we use

We use Supabase (database and sign-in), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications) and Google Sign-In. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app under Profile, then Delete account. This removes your account and personal data from our systems. You can also email us at the address below to request deletion.

Children

Turnova is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so users can only see what they are allowed to.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support.turnova@gmail.co
