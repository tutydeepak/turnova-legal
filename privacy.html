Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Turnova lets you join a barber shop or salon queue from your phone. Shop owners manage their queue from the Turnova shop dashboard. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect from customers

  • Account details: your name and email, or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google. If you sign up with email, we ask you to verify your email address.
  • Phone number: if you provide one, so the shop can call you if you are late. Only the owner of the shop you joined can see it. Barbers cannot.
  • Queue activity: the shops you join, services and barber you choose, and the times you join, are called, and finish.
  • Visits and points: your completed visits, points earned and rewards you redeem. Points do not expire.
  • Ratings and reviews: star ratings, the service you rated, and optional comments you submit after a visit.
  • Favourites and preferences: shops you favourite and the preferences you choose when you first open the app or later in Profile: the languages you speak, who you are booking for (men's, women's or everyone), your area or city, your search distance, and how many turns away you want to be alerted.
  • Device token: an identifier used to send you queue notifications.
  • Advertising data: to show ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See "Advertising" below.

What we collect from shop owners

If you register a shop, we collect your account details and the shop information you enter:

  • Shop name, address and map location, and whether the shop serves men, women or everyone.
  • Services offered, and the names and languages of the barbers you add.
  • An optional Google review link or Place ID for your shop.
  • Your contact details, and the branch name if you run a franchise. New shops are reviewed by the Turnova team before they are shown to customers, and the team may contact you using these details.

Shop information is shown to customers in the app so they can find and join your queue. Shop owners can also give barbers access with an invite code. A barber signs in and sees only their own chair: the customer's name and queue number, with controls to call, start and finish. Barbers cannot see customer phone numbers.

Shop owners can export their shop's monthly queue history as a CSV file. Customer phone numbers are included only if the owner turns that option on. Owners are responsible for keeping exported files safe.

Location

Location is optional. If you turn it on, the app uses your phone's location only to find and sort shops near you (within the distance you choose). Your location is never stored by us.

If you turn location off, you can type your area or city instead. That text is saved in your preferences and used to show shops in your area.

Notifications

We send push notifications about your place in a queue, such as when you are a set number of turns away and when it is your turn. Shops you have joined may also send offers, and a shop may send a request if a walk-in customer asks to go ahead of you (you can accept or decline). You can change how early you are alerted in your preferences, or turn notifications off in your phone settings.

Advertising

Turnova shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and other device information to deliver ads and measure how they perform, and it may use this to show ads based on your interests. We do not give AdMob your name, email, phone number or queue activity.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (usually under Privacy or Google, then Ads; the exact steps vary by device). Learn more in how Google uses data from ads.

How we use your information

  • To run the queue, show your position and estimated wait, and notify you when it is your turn.
  • To let shops manage their queue and contact you if you are late.
  • To show nearby shops, shop ratings, and your visit history.
  • To show shops and barbers that match your language and booking preferences.
  • To show ads in the app and keep Turnova running.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your account details or queue activity.

Who can see what

  • Other customers see a masked version of your name in a queue.
  • Shop owners see the names and details of people in their own shop's queue.
  • Reviews you write may be shown with your first name or a masked name.
  • Shop details, barber names and languages, and average ratings are visible to customers.
  • Shop owners can search the customers of their own shop by mobile number and see visit counts, points and redeemed rewards for that shop.
  • Barbers see only a customer's name and queue number.

Google reviews

Some shops add a link to their Google reviews. If you tap it, you leave Turnova and Google's privacy policy applies. We do not see what you write there.

Services we use

We use Supabase (database and sign-in), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), Firebase Hosting (the shop dashboard web app), Google Sign-In and Google AdMob (ads). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your Turnova account and data

We keep your data while your account is active.

Customers: you can delete your account in the app under Profile, then Delete account. This removes your account and personal data from our systems.

Shop owners: you can delete your account and shop together from the app. You will be asked for a reason. Deletion is scheduled with a recovery period of 30 days, during which your shop is hidden from customers. After that period, your account, shop and related data are permanently deleted.

You can also request deletion without opening the app: email us at the address below from the email you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Turnova account". We will delete your account and the data linked to it.

Children

Turnova is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so users can only see what they are allowed to.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
